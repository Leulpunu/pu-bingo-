Rules & Privacy

A guide to playing and how this version of PU Bingo uses your information.

Playing rules

  1. Choose one of 450 numbered cartelas during the 30-second selection countdown. Each cartela can belong to only one player in a room. Your selection stays reserved for later rounds in that room.
  2. The round starts automatically. Cards lock when play begins, and a number is called every four seconds.
  3. Mark called numbers manually or turn on Auto-mark. The center FREE space is already marked. B is gold, I green, N blue, G red, and O purple.
  4. Complete two distinct full lines on the same cartela (rows, columns, or diagonals), then tap Claim BINGO! With Auto ON, the app also submits your bingo claim automatically when you have two completed lines. With Auto OFF, tap Claim BINGO yourself. Keep the app open and connected for automatic claims. The server checks your card; the first valid claim received by the server wins.
  5. The winner earns 100 play points. Other players in that round earn 10. If nobody claims, the round ends after all 75 numbers have been displayed and each player earns 10 points.
  6. Results appear for eight seconds, then another 30-second selection period begins. Keep the app open to follow the draw. Games continue if you leave the screen.

Deposit simulator

In development mode, the Test wallet lets you submit fictional amounts and TEST references for administrator review. Requests, status, timestamps, and the reviewing account identifier are stored on the server separately from game profiles until the operator deletes them. Approved test amounts have no cash value. They can buy simulated cartelas in test-stake rooms, but cannot be withdrawn or redeemed for money. Stake, prize, refund, house-share, and round-settlement records are also saved on the server, including account IDs, cartela numbers, round IDs, amounts, and timestamps. Do not send money or enter real payment details.

Simulated withdrawals

You may request a withdrawal of test units only. No real Telebirr transfer is made. Enter a fictional TEST recipient label rather than personal payment details. The requested amount is reserved immediately. Administrator approval keeps it reserved; marking it simulated paid completes the test request. Rejection releases the reserved balance. The server retains your request ID, account ID, fictional recipient, amount, status, timestamps, reviewer IDs, rejection reasons, and simulated payout references with the test ledger. Pending requests remain reserved across server restarts.

Test-stake rounds

In development mode, a test-stake cartela costs 20 fictional units per round. Players may buy multiple cartelas at 20 test units each, with up to 450 cartelas available per room. Each purchased cartela is checked independently for two completed lines. Choose a card and explicitly buy it before the countdown ends; purchase locks that card. At least two paid players are required. Only players with purchased cards may claim the prize. The winner receives 80% of the total paid stakes; the house receives 20%. For 91 paid cards, the total is 1,820 test units, winner 1,456, house 364. If there is no winner or fewer than two paid players, all stakes are refunded and the house receives nothing. Unfinished stakes are refunded on server restart. New rounds require a new purchase; there is no automatic rebuy. Play points are separate from the test wallet.

Play points only

Free practice and friends rooms have no entry fees. The test wallet and test-stake rooms use fictional units only; this version has no real deposits, paid entry fees, withdrawals, purchases, or cash prizes. Points have no cash value and cannot be redeemed for money.

Who runs the game

PU Bingo is a third-party game operated independently of Telegram. Telegram provides the Mini App platform; it does not run the bingo rounds or award PU Bingo points. Game and privacy questions belong with the PU Bingo operator.

Information used by PU Bingo

  • Telegram sign-in: the server receives and checks Telegram's signed launch data, which may include your Telegram ID, name, username, language, and profile details. This version keeps your Telegram ID and first name in your game profile.
  • Game records: your profile includes play points, wins, and completed-round counts. These records are saved on the server. Rooms, assigned cards, and sign-in sessions are held in server memory.
  • Visible to other players: room members see player names and Telegram IDs, the winner's cartela, and the called numbers. The leaderboard shows player names, points, and win counts.
  • Browser preview: a random guest identifier is saved in this browser's local storage to recognize your guest profile. Clearing browser storage removes this identifier; it does not delete records on the server.
  • Connection data: the server and hosting provider receive normal web connection information, including your IP address. The game code does not save IP addresses to player profiles; hosting logs depend on the hosting provider's configuration.

Why the game uses this information

PU Bingo uses your player identifier and name to recognize your account, reserve cartelas, check room membership, and display players. Game records support rewards, profile statistics, and the leaderboard. Signed Telegram launch data is checked to authenticate your account and prevent impersonation. The current game code does not sell player data or use it for advertising.

Security and storage limits

The server checks Telegram signatures and session expiry, restricts room actions to room members, and validates cards and bingo claims. Profile records are stored in a server-side JSON file; this version does not encrypt that file itself. Production hosting must provide HTTPS and restrict access to server files. These measures reduce risk but cannot guarantee that interruptions or unauthorized access will never occur.

Game display name

The bot command /change_username lets you set a game display name. It is saved in your server profile and shown to other players and on the leaderboard. It does not change your Telegram username. Support and agent commands show contacts configured by the operator.

Optional phone sharing in the bot

If you choose Share my phone in a private chat with the PU Bingo bot, it receives your phone number and checks that the shared contact belongs to your Telegram account. The number and update timestamp are saved with your Telegram ID in a separate server file for the registration record. Sharing is optional; use /skip to play without it. Use /forgetphone to remove the stored phone record. Deleting it from PU Bingo does not delete your contact message from Telegram. Your phone is not displayed in game rooms or used to verify payments. Other bot messages, including pasted receipts, are not saved by the bot code or used to credit deposits. Telegram retains chat messages under its own policies.

External services

The app loads Telegram's Mini App script and Google Fonts. Your browser contacts those services, which receive connection information under their own policies. This version does not request your phone number, contacts, or location and does not include advertising or analytics tools.

Storage, access, and removal

Game profiles currently remain on the server until the operator removes them; there is no automatic deletion period or in-app deletion tool. Sign-in sessions expire after one hour. Active rooms and sessions reset if the server restarts. Contact the operator through the bot or channel where you opened PU Bingo for profile access or removal requests. A dedicated support contact has not yet been configured.

Your data requests

You can ask the PU Bingo operator for a copy of your stored profile, correction of inaccurate information, or deletion. You can also ask about restricting or objecting to processing, or withdrawing any consent you previously gave. Available rights and any permitted retention exceptions depend on applicable law. Stop using the app if you no longer want it to receive new launch and game data.

Use the operator's bot or channel where you opened the app to make a request. Include enough information to identify your profile, but never send your bot token, password, or Telegram sign-in code. The operator may need to verify that the request relates to your account. If you believe your data rights have not been respected, you may contact the relevant data protection authority.

Policy updates

This notice describes the current version of PU Bingo. Changes to data use, service providers, or storage should be reflected here with an updated date. Review this screen when the app changes.

Availability and fair play

Do not manipulate requests, impersonate other players, or interfere with rounds. A connection failure may delay what you see. Server restarts can interrupt games, and play points are for entertainment only.
